# ⬇ GrabbiExtendo - Mechanical lazy-tongs grabber with jaw meant for tool-gripping!
Designed in Fusion 360 for Week 2 of Hack Club's Half life program

Fully mechanical(Not fully functional yet!), extending grabber, mainly uses the lazy-tongs linkage, for reach. It also includes a geared 4bar jaw at the tip. The handles allow the linkage to extend by squeezing them and releasing retracts it. The jaw is useful for gripping small tools with scissor-like handles, such as pruning shears.
# Where did the idea come from?
More like how did the idea develop, I was actually planning on developing this a long time ago since I was 8, it looked like a fun tool. And thanks to the program I'm now developing it(While thinking of what to make I also saw a DIY tutorial on how to make an extending grabber) it also served as inspiration. The idea of it being able to hold pruning shears came from my grandma too, since she struggles to prune the branches high up in the trees.
# What's it useful for?
It has two fingers, you can grip stuff like cups with em, anything big enough that only needs two fingers to be carried. However, the main intended use of the GrabbiExtendo is to help elder people prune their trees, they just attach pruning shears to the claw and trigger the Lazy-tongs mechanism to reach the leaves and pull on the lever to cut, easy to use and doesn't need much strength.

## How it work
1. User squeezes the handles with two hands and the chain stretches away, reaching far depending on how long the linkage is
2. Now that the jaws are far, trigger the lever on one of the handles and a cable will be pulled
3. The cable is connected with the jaw gear, thus both spur gears rotate, thaks to a four-bar linkage
4. The four-bar linkage controls the fingers and they curl to squeeze the grip of pruning shears and cut, the TPU helps with the grip.
5. Pull on the lever, and the fingers are back into their original position, you can now release the handles to safely store the GrabbiExtendo.
